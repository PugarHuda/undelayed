Why the raw getters mislead

The limiter is not a tumbling window. Each elapsed window subtracts one whole limit from the recorded total instead of resetting it, so overspend carries forward and allowedAt = windowStart + windowSize × minted / max. Between writes the stored window start goes stale, which is why "on-chain reads" and "actually used" disagree above. A mint at or above the large-mint threshold skips both limiters entirely and consumes no window quota.
